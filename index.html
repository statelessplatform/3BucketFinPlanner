How the 3 Bucket Strategy Works

A simple, powerful framework for financial security at any life stage

What is the 3 Bucket Strategy?

The 3 Bucket Strategy divides your money into three pools — each with a different time horizon and risk level. Money flows from the high-growth Bucket 3 into Bucket 2, then into your daily Bucket 1.

Why it works: You never sell stocks during a crash. Buckets 1 & 2 keep you funded for 5+ years, giving Bucket 3 time to recover.

Understanding Each Bucket

Bucket 1 — Liquid Safety (24 months)

Where: Savings account, liquid MFs, FD

Returns: ~2% — low, but instantly accessible

Who: Everyone. Students keep emergency funds here. Retirees keep 2 years of income here.

Bucket 2 — Buffer Fund (36 months)

Where: Balance Advantage Funds, hybrid debt-equity

Returns: ~6–8% with lower volatility than equity

Who: Anyone saving for medium-term goals — home, education, car.

Bucket 3 — Growth Engine (Long Term)

Where: Equity mutual funds, direct stocks, index funds

Returns: ~11–15% — higher, with market volatility

Who: Everyone. Even ₹500/month SIP compounds massively over time.

Frequently Asked Questions

I'm a student with very little savings. Is this useful?
Yes. Enter small amounts — even ₹50,000 corpus and ₹5,000/month expenses. See how disciplined saving compounds over 20–30 years.
I'm a homemaker managing household finances.
Enter total family savings as corpus and total monthly household outflow as expenses. Plan for emergencies, children's education, or long-term goals.
What happens when Bucket 1 runs out?
The simulation auto-triggers a refill from Bucket 2, and if needed, from Bucket 3. In real life, you would redeem from the appropriate fund at that time.
Are these returns realistic?
The simulation uses actual historical Indian market returns from 2001–2026, including crashes in 2008, 2011, and 2020. The strategy is designed to survive bad years.
Is this financial advice?
No. Educational tool only. For actual investment decisions, consult a SEBI-registered financial advisor.
